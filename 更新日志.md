# 更新日志 / Changelog

本文件是 TerraFirmaEarth 的**完整**版本更新记录，仅作项目内记录、**不随包发布**。`mods.toml` 的介绍页不放日志，只保留功能总览（日志里仍在生效的改动已并入功能列表），逐条细节以本文件为准。
This file is the **complete** release history of TerraFirmaEarth. It is a project-internal record and is **not shipped** with the release. The mod description deliberately carries no changelog section, only a feature overview; the per-version details below are the source of truth.

## 中文

### 2.2.3 更新概括（未发布，版本号尚未在 `build.gradle` 提升）

- 树叶季节配色对齐 TFC 1.21：新增“常绿气候”判定（暖湿地区全年常绿）与“干湿季”判定（热区按旱季落叶），春夏季改用“年内进度 × 地下水”取色，冬季统一为褐色。
- 在此基础上保留“高山提前入秋”：季节判定仍按海拔修正的年均温计算，比 1.21 更贴近现实。
- 树叶配色改用自带的 1.21 色图。副作用：材质包对树叶的“调色”不再生效（树叶贴图、树叶模型与秋季配色不受影响）。
- 兼容：林海物语（AFC）的树叶，破坏与飘落粒子现在会带季节色。
- 修复：补回树叶随机刻被漏掉的原版行为（转发到 `tick()`），消除极少数“悬空树叶 / 延迟衰败”的情况。
- 自然刷新的鱼类新增“水域体积”门槛：刷新点所在的连通水域不足 100 格时不再刷新，避免鱼出现在水坑、小水洼这类不真实的位置；一条通向开阔水域的窄水道仍然算连通（鱼从外面游进来）。判定只统计真正的蓄水（源头与生成时使用的水位档），玩家放水 / 破堤摊开形成的浅水不算数。这是按生物单独开启的 fauna 数据包字段 `min_water_volume`（不写的生物不受影响），写 `0` 即关闭；刷怪笼、刷怪蛋等非自然来源也不受影响。
- Jade：玉米、甘蔗这类双格作物，以及高大的双格植物，对准上半部分查看时也会显示与下半部分一致的完整信息；在此之前只有下半部分能读到生长进度、健康、预计成熟与肥料等数据。
- 修复四种新增土壤（冲积土 / 黑土 / 灰化土 / 氧化土）的泥砖流程：泥砖与湿润泥砖之前挖掉不掉落（战利品表缺失，且泥砖不在铲子可挖标签里），现在用铲子挖会掉自身、湿砖堆会按数量掉出湿砖或干燥泥砖；湿润泥砖重新有了风干提示（下雨无法风干 / 还需多久 / 已风干）；新土壤的泥现在可以制作抹灰，干燥泥砖可以制作平滑泥砖；创造模式的地球标签里也能找到这四种土壤的整套方块。
- 修复木柴堆的外观：开启“动态资源加载”（ModernFix `dynamic_resources`）时，木柴堆与燃烧中的木柴堆会一直显示成满满一堆原木、不再随放入数量变化；现在重新按数量逐层显示。同时补全木柴堆的交互：林海物语（AFC）的木头（含“古”变体，例如古橡木）也能放进木柴堆；下蹲 + 空手右键可以一次取走一整堆，与下蹲 + 手持木头一次放入一整堆对称。

### 2.2.2 更新概括

- 补齐了原版缺失的“采摘休眠期”（TFC 1.21 本体自带 `fruitPickBloomDelayTicks`）：玩家采摘成熟果实后，果树树叶与浆果灌木在一段时间内不能再次开花结果。期间 Jade 直接显示“采摘休眠期：剩余 …”，天数由 `harvest_dormancy` 配置段控制（默认 10 天，0 = 关闭）。香蕉采摘后即刻枯萎死亡，不在此周期内。
- 受控温室内的“无视季节、跨季节结果”统一收归本模组实现（含果树树苗），读取 TFCModernLife 提供的受控温度与温室判定；该模组不再需要自行接管植物生命周期。
- 修复了一代温室模组（`firmalife_greenhouse_patch` / FirmalifeEnhance）兼容失效的问题。
- 农夫乐事的沃土耕地，以及“幸存者的沃土扩展”的五档沃土耕地，现在都会按档位获得土壤肥力加成（最高档相当于比黑土再多 10%），越肥沃的土壤越省肥料；同时沃土耕地可以直接对空白耕地施肥，不必先种上作物。
- Jade：预计距成熟现在会计入土壤肥力与土壤中已有的养分；并新增一行肥料提示——肥料够用时显示“肥料充足”，不够时显示“完美成熟还需 X% 的氮 / 磷 / 钾肥”。
- 帕秋莉手册：土壤肥力章节重排为八种土壤（新成土 / 火山土 / 冲积土 / 淋溶土 / 黑土 / 灰化土 / 旱成土 / 氧化土）并补齐各档位说明；兼容功能章节新增农夫乐事与“生存者的沃土扩展”两页；“更细致的信息显示”章节补充了肥料提示的说明。

## English

### 2.2.3 Update Summary (unreleased; the `build.gradle` version has not been raised yet)

- Seasonal foliage colours now follow TFC 1.21: added the "evergreen climate" rule (warm and wet areas keep their leaves all year) and the dry-season rule (hot areas shed leaves in their dry season); spring and summer now sample "time of year x groundwater", and winter uses one uniform brown.
- "Earlier autumn on high ground" is kept on top of that: the season window is still decided from the elevation adjusted average temperature, which is closer to reality than 1.21.
- Leaf colours now use a bundled 1.21 colormap. Side effect: a resource pack's leaf *tinting* no longer applies (leaf textures, leaf models and autumn colours are unaffected).
- Compatibility: ArborFirmaCraft (AFC) leaves now give seasonal colours to their break and falling leaf particles.
- Fix: restored the vanilla behaviour the leaf random tick had lost (forwarding to `tick()`), removing rare cases of floating or late decaying leaves.
- Naturally spawned fish now require a connected body of water of at least 100 blocks at the spawn position, so they no longer appear in puddles and tiny ponds; a narrow channel that reaches open water still counts as connected (the fish swam in from outside). Only real standing water counts (sources and the single flowing level world generation uses); shallow water spread by players or by a broken bank does not. This is a per-creature fauna data field, `min_water_volume` (creatures that do not declare it are unaffected), and `0` disables the check. Spawners, spawn eggs and other non-natural sources are unaffected.
- Jade: two-block crops such as maize and sugarcane, and tall two-block plants, now show the same complete information from their upper half as from their lower half; previously only the lower half could read growth, health, estimated time to maturity and fertilizer.
- Fixed the mud brick chain of the four new soils (Fluvisol / Mollisol / Podzol / Oxisol): mud bricks and wet bricks previously dropped nothing (the loot tables were missing and the bricks were not in the shovel tag), and now shovels drop the block itself while a wet brick stack drops the right number of wet bricks or dried mud bricks; wet bricks show a drying status again (raining, time left, dried); the new soils' mud can be used for daub and their dried bricks for smooth mud bricks; and the whole set is now found in the creative Earth tab.
- Fixed the log pile visuals: with ModernFix's dynamic resources enabled the log pile and the burning log pile were stuck on a full pile of logs and no longer followed the number of logs inside; they now show the correct layer per log again. The log pile interactions were also completed: ArborFirmaCraft (AFC) logs, including the "ancient" variants such as ancient oak, can be put into a log pile, and sneak-right-clicking with an empty hand now takes a whole pile at once, mirroring sneak + a held log inserting a whole pile.

### 2.2.2 Update Summary

- Restored the vanilla-missing harvest dormancy (TFC 1.21 ships it as `fruitPickBloomDelayTicks`): after a player harvests ripe fruit, fruit tree leaves and berry bushes cannot bloom again for a while. Jade reports "Harvest dormancy: ... left"; the duration is controlled by the `harvest_dormancy` config section (10 days by default, 0 disables it). Banana plants wither and die on harvest, so this cooldown does not apply to them.
- Controlled-greenhouse season immunity (fruiting regardless of season, saplings included) is now implemented entirely by this mod, reading the controlled temperature and greenhouse check from TFCModernLife; that mod no longer needs to take over the plant lifecycle.
- Fixed the first-generation greenhouse mod (`firmalife_greenhouse_patch` / FirmalifeEnhance) compatibility failing.
- Farmer's Delight rich soil farmland and the five Survivor's Rich Soils farmland variants now receive soil fertility by tier (the top tier is 10% above Mollisol), and richer soil needs less fertilizer. Rich soil farmland can also be fertilized directly while empty, without planting a crop first.
- Jade: the estimated time to maturity now accounts for soil fertility and the nutrients already stored in the soil, and a new fertilizer line shows either "Fertilizer sufficient" or "Perfect maturity needs X% more nitrogen / phosphorus / potassium fertilizer".
- Field guide: the Soil Fertility chapter was reworked into eight soils (Entisol / Andisol / Fluvisol / Alfisol / Mollisol / Podzol / Aridisol / Oxisol) with per-tier notes; Compatibility Features gained pages for Farmer's Delight and Survivor's Rich Soils; More Detailed Information now documents the fertilizer line.
