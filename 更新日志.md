## 中文

### 2.2.2 更新概括

- 为 TFC 1.20 补齐了原版缺失的“采摘休眠期”（TFC 1.21 本体自带 `fruitPickBloomDelayTicks`）：玩家采摘成熟果实后，果树树叶与浆果灌木在一段时间内不能再次开花结果。期间 Jade 直接显示“采摘休眠期：剩余 …”，不再显示“展叶期”等生命周期状态；天数由 `harvest_dormancy` 配置段控制（默认 10 天，0 = 关闭）。香蕉采摘后即刻枯萎死亡，不在此周期内。
- 受控温室内的“无视季节、跨季节结果”统一收归本模组实现（含果树树苗），读取 TFCModernLife 提供的受控温度与温室判定；该模组不再需要自行接管植物生命周期。

### 2.2.1 更新概括

- 修复了沉浸式飞机无法有效识别群峦水体的问题：有浮力的飞机不再下沉，并会随流动的河水一并漂移。
- 重新调整了海洋的深度，现在海洋全局加深，越远越深，具体请查看帕秋莉。

### 2.2.0 更新概括

## English

### 2.2.2 Update Summary

- Restored the vanilla-missing harvest dormancy for TFC 1.20 (TFC 1.21 ships it as `fruitPickBloomDelayTicks`): after a player harvests ripe fruit, fruit tree leaves and berry bushes cannot bloom again for a while. Jade reports "Harvest dormancy: ... left" instead of the leaf/flowering lifecycle state during that window; the duration is controlled by the `harvest_dormancy` config section (10 days by default, 0 disables it). Banana plants wither and die on harvest, so this cooldown does not apply to them.
- Controlled-greenhouse season immunity (fruiting regardless of season, saplings included) is now implemented entirely by this mod, reading the controlled temperature and greenhouse check from TFCModernLife; that mod no longer needs to take over the plant lifecycle.

### 2.2.1 Update Summary

- Fixed Immersive Aircraft failing to recognize TFC water bodies: buoyant aircraft no longer sink, and they drift along with flowing river water.
- Readjusted ocean depth: oceans are now globally deeper, getting deeper the farther they are from land; see the field guide for details.

### 2.2.0 Update Summary

- Substantially reworked the rope anchor system: more realistic, and far more valuable in the early and mid game.
- Added the underground sections of stream biomes.

### 2.1.0 Update Summary

- Ported the latest TFC terrain, climate, fauna, flora, and related content.
- Ported the soil fertility settings.
- Optimized terrain generation across all terrain types to reduce terrain-damaging probabilities, making the overall terrain cleaner, flatter, and more consistent.
- Added ropes and related interactions.
- Added full crop-state information to Jade and introduced crop health.
- Improved the prospecting system and adjusted fruit-tree climate conditions.
- Improved and expanded the TFE Patchouli field guide with organized entries for each feature.
- See the Patchouli field guide for detailed mechanics and parameters.

### 2.0.2 Update Summary

- Added crop health: crops stop growing and gradually lose health when temperature or hydration is unsuitable, then recover once the climate becomes suitable again. Crops die when health reaches zero, or when the temperature exceeds the suitable range by 10°C. See the crop chapter of the TFE Patchouli field guide for details.
- Added crop health to the Jade information panel.
- Banana harvests now yield 8 - 16 bananas, and each banana provides 1.5 fruit and 0.5 dairy nutrition; directly breaking the top of a mature plant now drops 1 - 3 saplings.
- Fixed missing middle-section drops when arundo, dry phragmite or tree ferns are broken from the bottom.

### 2.0.1 Update Summary

- Added brand-new streams to river biomes.
- Reduced gravel on high river-biome banks.
- Fixed a crash that could occur when dying with no item drops while holding a hammer and chisel and aiming at a stone block.
- Reworked the prospecting system; see the Patchouli field guide for details.
- Reworked boat buoyancy, allowing boats to travel upward with flowing water.
- Reworked climate requirements for all crops; see the Patchouli field guide for details.
- Reworked the global temperature algorithm; see the Patchouli field guide for details.
- Improved the TFE Patchouli field guide.
- Fixed several other issues.

### 1.1.3 Update Summary

- Replaced ocean mud seabeds with a thinner gravel surface layer.
- Marked this mod's items registered under the tfc namespace as TFE-provided, so searching @tfe in JEI now shows all items from this mod.

### 1.1.2 Update Summary

- Fixed terrain generation issues around coastal beaches and related shore terrain.
- Optimize the terrain selection for volcanoes
- Improved cassava nutrition values.
- Aligned berry bush and fruit tree growth conditions with TFC 1.21, and changed their temperature checks to use current temperature.
- Unsuitable climate no longer kills berry bushes or fruit tree saplings; affected plants now stop growing instead.
- TFCModernLife (群峦现代化生活) compatibility: greenhouse control is based on current temperature. It is recommended as the greenhouse, cellar and electric kitchen expansion for this mod; do not enable duplicate greenhouse expansions at the same time.

### 1.1.1 Update Summary

- Fixed naturally generated banana plants sometimes sinking into terrain.
- Fixed tuff gravel pan stage model rendering.
- Fixed client-side rendering offsets for stove pot fluid, outputs and contents.
- Improved radishes: cultivated mature harvest yield is doubled.
- Adjusted average hydration display: reference values no longer show too high / too low warnings.

### 1.1.0 Update Summary

- Optimized terrace terrain.
- Fixed mountain river mouths sometimes being blocked at the sea.
- Smoothed mountain peak generation.
